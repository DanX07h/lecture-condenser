SLIDES TO NOTES

Bring your decks together

Turn multiple PowerPoints into one focused study guide. Repeated ideas are merged; key concepts stay highlighted.

Select one or more decks. For older .ppt files, save as .pptx first. Images and diagrams are not interpreted.

Files are temporarily stored during processing, deleted after the request, and sent to your configured AI provider (Groq or OpenAI) when you generate notes. Only upload material you have permission to share. This prototype is for local development; it has no user accounts or access controls.
